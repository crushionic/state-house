To hold forth a lively experiment: how to launch

Try it on your computer
  1. Open a terminal in this folder.
  2. Run:   python3 -m http.server 8000
  3. Open:  http://localhost:8000/          (the walk, ending in the library)
            http://localhost:8000/book.html (the book page)
  Double-clicking index.html will NOT work: browsers block the splat files that way.

Put it online
  Upload this whole folder, as is, to any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, etc.).
  No build step. index.html is the front page.
  It needs an internet connection: three.js and Spark load from CDNs, fonts from Google Fonts.

Not included (on purpose)
  splats/catalog-card.spz  your team's card scan; a stand-in card shows until you add it.

Authoring
  Add ?dev to the address (e.g. http://localhost:8000/?dev) for the camera tools.
  In ?dev, type  site.jumpTo(1)  in the browser console to jump to the library.
  Full details: README.md
